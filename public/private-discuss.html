Before your demo

Start with
the right questions.

OpenGate helps you frame the use case and the commercial conversation.

How does OpenGate help?

We introduce Private Discuss around your business needs, coordinate a product discussion and help identify the questions your IT and procurement teams need answered.

Can we keep it on our own servers?

Private Discuss offers on-premise deployment as well as SaaS. We can arrange a discussion of the infrastructure, network and administration requirements with the product team.

What should our IT team evaluate?

Start with deployment location, access controls, integration requirements, retention policies and the features your teams actually need. Confirm the security architecture and available options for your chosen deployment with Private Discuss.

Can it fit into our existing applications?

Private Discuss provides APIs and SDKs for integration. Bring your identity system, collaboration workflows and application requirements to the demo so compatibility can be assessed.

Are these live customer screens?

No. These are public product illustrations supplied on the Private Discuss website. A tailored demo is the right next step to see the product in use and confirm the features in your proposed deployment.
