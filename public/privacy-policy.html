Privacy policy.

A plain-language summary of the information this website uses and the services that may process it.

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Who is responsible for this website

OpenGate Advisory (FZE), Sharjah Research Technology & Innovation Park (SRTIP), Block B – B57-089, License N° 11426, United Arab Emirates, is responsible for this website and for personal information it receives directly.

Information we may receive

We may receive information that you choose to provide when arranging a discovery call or contacting us, such as your name, work contact details, company, preferred meeting time and the information you include in a message. We also receive limited technical and usage information needed to operate, protect and understand the website.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate website activity such as page views, routes, devices and approximate visitor geography. The site also contains privacy-conscious Google Analytics 4 integration that is activated only when a valid OpenGate measurement property is configured. Where analytics is active, browser settings, content blockers and applicable consent requirements may limit collection.

Third-party services

Discovery-call links open Microsoft Outlook Bookings, which processes booking information under Microsoft’s own terms and privacy practices. The real-estate page may display an OpenStreetMap map, and portfolio links may open external websites. Those providers receive information necessary to deliver their service, such as your IP address and browser request.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, arrange meetings, evaluate potential engagements, improve the clarity and performance of this website, maintain security and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was provided, an active business relationship, or a legal and accounting requirement. We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask what personal information we hold, request correction or deletion, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent. You can also limit analytics through browser privacy controls or a content blocker.

Contact and changes

To raise a privacy question or request, use the contact or discovery-call link on this website and identify the request as privacy-related. We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change; the date above shows the latest revision.
