Getting started should feel straightforward.

What does OpenGate help companies do?

We help you enter new markets, find partners and build a practical sales approach. You get experienced commercial support without having to build a full local team first.

Where do you work?

Across Southern Europe, the Middle East, Africa and Southeast Asia, with the UAE as our base. The right approach depends on your target market and the expertise it needs.

Do you only work with technology companies?

Technology is our core background. We also support businesses in other sectors where local knowledge, relationships and commercial execution matter.

How do we get started?

Start with a discovery call. We’ll discuss your business, the market you’re considering and what’s holding you back, then suggest a focused first step.

When will I hear back?

We aim to respond to new enquiries within one business day.
