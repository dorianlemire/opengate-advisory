What is Morbit Studio?

A cloud platform that brings connected workplace devices, meeting-room health, alerts and operational trends into one management view.

Who is it for?

IT directors, CTOs, workplace teams and service partners responsible for keeping technology reliable across one or more locations.

We’ll review your devices, systems and requirements together. Supported integrations and the scope of an evaluation should be confirmed for your specific estate.
