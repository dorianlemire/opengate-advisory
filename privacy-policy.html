Your information

Privacy policy.

This policy explains how Bellevue Residencies (Private) Limited may collect and use information when you visit this website or ask for project information. Last updated: 17 September 2026.

Information you provide

When you submit an enquiry, we may receive your name, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, country of residence, property interest and message. If the website's direct form endpoint has not yet been connected, the form prepares a WhatsApp message for you to review and send yourself; the website does not silently store that message.

How information is used

We use enquiry information to respond, arrange presentations, provide requested sales material, understand buyer requirements and keep a record of communications. We do not sell personal data.

Analytics and cookies

When a valid Google Analytics 4 measurement ID is configured, Google Analytics may collect device, browser, approximate location, page and interaction information. IP anonymisation is requested in the website configuration. Google may set or read analytics cookies. Analytics is not loaded while the measurement ID remains unconfigured.

WhatsApp and external links

If you choose WhatsApp, Google Maps or another external service, that provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Review the message and destination before sending personal details.

Retention and security

Enquiry records should be retained only as long as needed for the sales relationship, legal obligations and legitimate business records. Reasonable administrative and technical controls should be used, but no internet transmission is entirely risk-free.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, or ask not to receive marketing messages. Contact the project team using the public number below. Legal rights vary by location.
