Where exactly is Bellevue Residencies?

The registered project address is 40 Negombo Road, Tudella, Ja-Ela, in Sri Lanka's Western Province. It sits within the Colombo–Katunayake airport corridor.

What residence types are planned?

Published project material describes 2, 3 and 4 bedroom apartments together with penthouses. The interactive plans on this website are illustrative; request the official sales release for final areas and layouts.

Is the project currently under construction?

Yes. NCD Consultants currently lists the 35-storey luxury apartment complex as in progress and at the transfer-floor construction stage. Construction status can change, so request a dated update before making a purchase decision.

What amenities are planned?

Published plans include an infinity swimming pool, gymnasium, landscaped recreational areas and solar-powered common areas. A supermarket, food court and shops were proposed for a later commercial phase in the original masterplan.

How close is the airport?

Ja-Ela is approximately 15 km by road from Bandaranaike International Airport. Typical journey estimates are around 12–20 minutes, but traffic and the exact route can materially change travel time.

Can overseas Sri Lankans buy?

Sri Lankan citizens living abroad can generally buy residential property, subject to standard legal and banking checks. Foreign ownership rules are different and can change; obtain independent legal and tax advice for your circumstances.

What return can an investor expect?

No return is guaranteed. Yield depends on purchase price, rent, vacancy, service charges, finance, tax and exit value. Use the editable model above as a starting point, then validate assumptions with independent advisers and current comparable evidence.

When will prices and payment plans be available?

Pricing, availability and payment stages should be taken only from the current official sales pack. Register your interest below to request the latest release.
