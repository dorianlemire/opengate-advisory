What is Morbit Studio?

Morbit Studio is a cloud platform that brings connected workplace technology, meeting-room health, device status, alerts and operational trends into one management view.

Who is Morbit designed for?

It is designed for CTOs, IT directors, workplace technology teams, building operators and service partners responsible for reliable multi-site technology estates.

What problem does Morbit solve?

Morbit replaces fragmented monitoring and reactive support with a shared operating picture that helps teams prioritise issues, understand trends and improve service conversations.

Can Morbit support multiple sites?

The cloud management model is designed to give authorised teams and service partners visibility across distributed locations from one accessible view.
