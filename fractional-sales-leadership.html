Fractional leadership is not occasional advice. It is direct involvement in the decisions, rhythms and opportunities that determine whether a sales organisation moves forward.

01 · Focus

Commercial priorities

Clarify segments, territories, offers and account priorities so effort follows a coherent revenue thesis.

02 · Visibility

Pipeline and forecasting

Create common qualification standards, inspect risk and distinguish genuine progress from hopeful reporting.

03 · Rhythm

Sales operating cadence

Build useful weekly and monthly reviews around decisions, obstacles and commitments rather than administration.

04 · Conversion

Coaching and deal strategy

Strengthen discovery, value articulation, stakeholder coverage, negotiation and the path to a credible next step.

When it fits

Leadership before
permanent headcount.

This model suits businesses that have a credible offer and a meaningful opportunity, but do not yet need—or cannot yet justify—a full-time regional CRO. It also helps established teams through a market-entry phase, leadership transition or period of inconsistent execution.

The objective is not dependency. OpenGate brings structure, judgement and hands-on support while strengthening the people, processes and commercial evidence the organisation will need for its next stage.
