Who we are

OpenGate Advisory (FZE) is responsible for this website and the personal information it receives for its business activities. Our registered office is Sharjah Research Technology & Innovation Park (SRTIP), Block B - B57-089, United Arab Emirates. Our licence number is 11426.

For enquiries or privacy requests, email hello@opengate-advisory.com. You do not need to book a call to exercise your privacy rights.

Information we receive and why

Enquiries and meetings. If you email us or book a call, we receive the information you provide, such as your name, business email, company, message and meeting preferences. We use it to respond, arrange meetings and discuss or deliver an engagement. Please do not send sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for your enquiry.

Website delivery and security. Our hosting provider processes technical request information, which may include an IP address, browser information and requested pages, to deliver the site, diagnose faults and protect the service. This operational processing is separate from optional analytics.

Optional analytics. If you allow it, Vercel Web Analytics helps us understand page visits, device and browser categories, approximate location and clicks on our booking and email links. These clicks indicate interest; they do not confirm that a message was sent or an appointment booked. We do not include the content of enquiries in these events. Our analytics integration removes URL query strings and fragments from page-view URLs.

The basis for using information

Where applicable data-protection law requires a lawful basis, we use information to take steps you request before an engagement or to perform a contract; to meet legal obligations; and, where permitted, for our legitimate interests in handling business correspondence and operating a secure website. Optional website analytics is based on your consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time through Privacy settings.

Cookies and browser storage

Optional analytics is off until you select Allow analytics or enable it in Privacy settings. Rejecting analytics does not restrict access to any page. We do not currently use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or embedded social-media trackers.

Your privacy choice: we save whether you allow analytics, the date of your choice and the preference version in your browser under opengate-privacy. The choice is valid for 180 days, after which we ask again on your next visit. This preference is not an advertising identifier.

Your theme: if you use the light/dark switch, we save the selected theme under opengate-theme. It remains until you change the preference or clear your browser's site data. Light mode is the default.

Vercel analytics: Vercel describes its measurement as using a request-based hash rather than third-party cookies, with visitor-session identifiers discarded after 24 hours. That period is distinct from how long aggregate statistics may be retained. We nevertheless ask for your permission before loading optional analytics. See Vercel's analytics privacy information.

Use Privacy settings in the footer to change your choice. Withdrawing permission stops future optional analytics and may reload the page to unload the analytics software. It does not undo processing that already occurred. Clearing site data removes saved preferences. If your browser blocks storage, your choice may only last for the current page. Without JavaScript, optional analytics stays off.

Service providers and external websites

Vercel hosts this website and provides optional analytics. Microsoft Bookings handles discovery-call bookings when you follow a booking link, and email providers process messages sent to us. We use information received through these services to handle your request. Provider processing and any cookies on their own websites are also described in their privacy notices: Vercel and Microsoft.

Links to LinkedIn, technology partners and portfolio websites open external services. Their privacy practices apply when you visit them. Product pictures and portfolio previews on this site are hosted locally; viewing them does not load the external platforms. We do not sell personal information.

International processing

OpenGate is based in the United Arab Emirates. Our hosting, booking and email providers may process information in other countries, including where their infrastructure and support teams operate. The destination and any required transfer safeguards depend on the service and applicable law. Contact us for information about the arrangements relevant to your enquiry.

How long information is kept

We keep enquiry and booking information for the period needed to respond, arrange meetings and manage the resulting business relationship. Records may be retained longer where necessary for legal or accounting obligations, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. The applicable period depends on the record and the relationship; you can ask us about your records using the email above.

Browser preferences have the lifetimes described above. Hosting logs and analytics records are also subject to the relevant provider's retention settings and service terms. Withdrawing analytics consent stops future optional collection; it does not identify or remove your contribution from already aggregated statistics.

Your rights and requests

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to access your personal information, correct it, request deletion or a portable copy, restrict processing, object to certain uses, and withdraw consent. Contact hello@opengate-advisory.com with enough information to identify your request. We may need to verify your identity before disclosing or changing records. We respond within the period required by applicable law.

You may also raise a complaint with the data-protection authority competent for your circumstances. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before it was withdrawn.

Updates

We update this notice when the website or our information-handling practices change. The date above identifies the current notice. Material changes to optional analytics will be reflected in the privacy controls before they take effect.
